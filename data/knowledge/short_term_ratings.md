# Short-Term Rating Scales (Synthetic Reference)

> Out of scope for the long-term ratings features (R1–R8). This document is included in the knowledge base to measure retrieval precision.

Short-term ratings describe the ability to meet obligations due within about 13 months.

| Moody's-style | Fitch-style | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| P-1 | F1+ / F1 | Superior / highest short-term capacity |
| P-2 | F2 | Strong / good short-term capacity |
| P-3 | F3 | Acceptable / adequate short-term capacity |
| NP | B, C | Not prime / speculative short-term |
| — | RD, D | Restricted default / default |

Short-term ratings are stored in a separate table and are validated by the Money Markets service, not the Ratings Data Entry component.
